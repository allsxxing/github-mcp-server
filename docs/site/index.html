04. Strategic Overview

Market Context & Operational Tools

Executive Summary

Squarespace Business is the optimal MVP vehicle for the East End market, balancing visual impact with low overhead. Migrating the asset (domain) to Cloudflare secures the brand's digital IP at wholesale cost.

View Squarespace 10-K Report

Risk Factors

  • Platform Lock-in: Squarespace prices historically rise every ~24 months.
  • Dependence: Traffic relies heavily on Meta (IG/FB) algorithms. The website serves purely as a validation/menu node.
Squarespace Investor Relations

Financial Tools
